    When a stack finishes

    Calibration

    Backlash is the play in the lead screw: after reversing, the motor turns this far before the carriage follows. Focus on a small, sharp detail, then measure: the rail passes through its focus both ways and compares.

    Scan module

    Offline
    Hall sensor
    –

    With no magnet near, the sensor reads about 512. Turn the head slowly by hand: near the magnet the reading moves well away from that.

    Calibration

    Yaw turn. Homes yaw, turns three times and homes again, to measure exactly how many steps make a turn. Nothing needs to be on the holder. About a minute.

    Tilt level. Clamp a straight pin upright in the holder, home the rail and the module, and focus on the middle of the pin. The module then tilts it 10° each way and compares where its top and bottom come into focus. Until this is done, tilt stops at −20° on the belly side.

    Motion

    Motor current

    The motor runs at full current while moving and stacking, and drops to the idle hold current after it has been idle for a while.

    Diagnostics

    Activity

      Which step size?

      Each shot is sharp over a thin slice: the depth of field. Stepping half of it per shot gives the slices a comfortable overlap. It depends on the lens's numerical aperture (NA, printed on microscope objectives) and the magnification on the sensor.
